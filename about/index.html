I'm Jason Harper, an economist and founder in the Detroit area.

I hold a BA and MA in Economics and an MBA. In 2016 I founded RXA, a data science firm in Ann Arbor; it was acquired by OneMagnify in 2023, where I serve as Founder / Managing Director. I'm also Founder and President of Ready Signal, an external-data platform for forecasting.

Today I run VYGO, doing production engineering for AI-built software — taking working prototypes and rebuilding them for production readiness, security, and handoff. On the side: Design Parenting, applying product thinking.
