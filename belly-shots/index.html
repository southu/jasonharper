Belly Shots

One of the many 'best' things about Hyperfit USA is the photos.  They document every WOD, every day.  And usually post them up to the Hyperfit Picasa web album within a few hours.  Much like tracking the numbers on Beyond The Whiteboard, looking at these pictures becomes extremely addictive.

A funny theme has started to emerge in my photos.  I call them the "Belly Shots".  It seems my shirts are just not long enough to cover my belly as I lift heavy things over my head.  So I've been keeping a record of them as they show up.  This seems like a fun way to track progress, as my main goal is to get this belly of mine back in line with my waist, as opposed to the nice shelf it had become.

I'll be updating this stream as more shots come on line.

All credit, photo and otherwise, goes to HyperfitUSA.com.
