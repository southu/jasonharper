CrossFit Shoes

I'll keep this simple.

Get the Inov-8 F-lite 195 Cross-Training Shoe

I have personally used 8 different shoes (plus going barefoot a few times) for CrossFit in my first year and a half:


  • Nike Air Max (2 types) - way too much heel, runners do not make good CrossFit shoes... or running shoes once I re-learned how to run

  • Vibram 5 Fingers - feel as funky as they look, just go real barefoot

  • Merrell Trail Gloves - too much toe grip, perfect for Tough Mudder

  • Merrell Road Gloves - pretty good, these are my second favorite

  • Inov-8 Bare X-F 210 - did not fit right, very uncomfortable

  • Inov-8 f-lite 230 - a lot of shoe, I wear these for my GORUCK events

  • Inov-8 f-lite 195 - the perfect CrossFit shoe


Like I said at the start, get the Inov-8 F-lite 195 Cross-Training Shoe

I wear a 10.5 in Nike, and I'm a perfect fit in a 10 in these shoes.  They are the perfect blend of light, minimal, supporting and of course, looking cool.
